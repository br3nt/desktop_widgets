This browser study uses simulated agent activity and Dock geometry. Dragging, snapping, grouping, size changes, drawers, and controls work here; native macOS click-through and Dock tracking belong to the app prototype.

Original Sonique references and what changed

The 1999/2000 screenshots from Sonique’s original overview page informed the figure-eight body, scooped shell edges, chrome thumb controls, inset screens, coloured lenses, and pull-out drawer. Capsules’s artwork is newly drawn.
