THE JOINT

A lip beneath a lip

Complementary grooves approach, then a pale lip slides beneath the other. The dark end caps withdraw; one stepped ink seam and a chalk peg remain. Click the peg, or focus it and press Enter, to release.

THE MATERIAL

Chalk above the deep

Swept shell ridges and short etched cuts surround a quiet blue-black well. Flat painted shadows give the plates their depth; a small rust-red woven pull breaks the cool surface.
