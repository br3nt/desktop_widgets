THE JOINT

A turn, then a quiet catch

The open copper spiral tilts through a hammered eye and turns back toward its stem. Daylight remains around the S-shaped bridge. Click the hook, or focus it and press Enter, to release.

THE MATERIAL

Uneven hoops, tightly wound collars and a wrapped green cabochon carry a patinated reading plate. Fine roots and a few unequal pearls sit outside the display.
