THE JOINT

A living line of silver

Split tips open along matching channels, then slide into one branching rib. Two enamel leaves close over the overlap. Click the pearl-sized collar to release the graft.

THE MATERIAL

Bows, enamel, open air

Chased silver bows taper into a crescent at the outer end. Pale celadon enamel marks the joint. Opaque blue-black plates keep every readout clear of the tracery.

THE EXPERIMENT

Six joins, three silver shades

Pressed leaf closes the bows into an almond. Quill sheath slides successive ribs beneath a chased end cap, leaving a long silver reading spine. Fan of ribs turns four chased ribs around an enamel pivot into a layered horizontal spine. Choose a treatment to play it; Expand reverses the motion.
