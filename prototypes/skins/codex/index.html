05 / HOLLOW · 9 BUILT OPTIONS

A quiet seam

Chalk shells meet through a stepped lap, crossing silk, a carved reed bolt, jagged shadows that lead their solid edges, a split seed husk secured by a dark waist band, or a silk suspension bridge across an open gap. Nested husks shade gathers three rigid plates beneath a readable dark slot; the red pull extends them again. Silk blind winds a woven membrane onto a pale rod and restores it with a red pull. Ink closure paints the shell into a brush-edged black summary plaque.
