Hollow Knight & Silksong Architecture & The Weaver's Silk Lash Join

Crafted from smooth, bone-white bleached chitin carapaces layered over deep subterranean slate frames. The silhouette features sculpted stag-beetle horn crests, insectoid mandible curvatures, and cold-forged iron nail blade grips. All displays sit in recessed slate embrasures with high-contrast bone-white typography and amber lumafly bioluminescence.

Option 1 — Weaver's Silk Lash (Tension Twang): Module A carries three cast Pharloom brass bobbins with wound golden silk thread; Module B features three polished bone eyelet studs. Upon docking or selecting Joining state, three glowing golden silk threads shoot across the divide, loop through the opposing bone eyelets, and cinch tight with high-speed tension twang. The joined seam features a taut criss-cross golden silk weave spanning the void between the chitin plates. Pulling the brass needle clapper right snaps the threads and separates the modules.

Shade Mode: The chitin plates compress into an ultra-compact 44px bone-white horn-crest diadem bar with glowing lumafly eye slits.
