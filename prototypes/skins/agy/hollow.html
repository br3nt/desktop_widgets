Hollow Knight & Silksong Architecture · Joins & Shade Modes

The silhouette features sculpted stag-beetle horn crests, insectoid mandible curvatures, and cold-forged iron nail blade grips. All displays sit in recessed slate embrasures with high-contrast bone-white typography and amber lumafly bioluminescence.

The joined seam features a taut criss-cross golden silk weave spanning the void between the chitin plates. Pulling the brass needle clapper right snaps the threads and separates the modules.

The widget title and status remain crystal-clear on the illuminated diadem plaque. Unwrapping releases the golden silk tension with a resonant twang, expanding the lower body back to its full instrument height.
