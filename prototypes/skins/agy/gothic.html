Gothic Basilica Architecture & The Keystone Drop Join

In this aesthetic, modules are carved from dressed ashlar limestone. Pointed lancet arches frame the clerestory displays, supported by fluted colonnettes. When separate, each module's mating end forms one half of a monumental pointed arch (a voussoir springer).

Option 1 — Keystone Drop (Nave Vault Key): As the two housings meet within the magnetic field, the voussoirs align. A carved granite keystone with an embossed trefoil and gothic cross descends vertically into the apex niche, locking the two independent modules into a continuous, monumental basilican arch. The release grip acts as a forged iron tie-wedge: pulling right unlocks the keystone.

Shade Mode: Collapses the stone structure into an architectural lintel frieze with a gothic status strip, preserving glanceable agent state.
