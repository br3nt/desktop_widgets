Gothic Basilica Architecture · Keystone Join, Portcullis Shade, Lead-Came Solder, Triptych Altar, Flying Buttress, Telescoping Pillars, Bronze Gargoyle & Rose Window Oculus

In this aesthetic, modules are carved from dressed ashlar limestone. Pointed lancet arches frame the clerestory displays, supported by fluted colonnettes. When separate, each module's mating end forms one half of a monumental pointed arch (a voussoir springer).

Option 1 — Keystone Drop (Nave Vault Key) [Join]: As the two housings meet within the magnetic field, the voussoirs align. A carved granite keystone with an embossed trefoil and gothic cross descends vertically into the apex niche, locking the two independent modules into a continuous, monumental basilican arch. The release grip acts as a forged iron tie-wedge: pulling right unlocks the keystone.

Option 2 — Lancet Portcullis Guillotine [Shade]: When collapsing to shade mode, a heavy forged-iron portcullis grille with barbed spike shoes drops vertically from behind the carved stone machicolations with gravity acceleration. The lower display bays slide upward behind the frieze, locking the widget into a fortified 44px architectural lintel. The status text and votive jewels remain cleanly visible and readable on the stone lintel plaque. Raising the portcullis winches the iron gate smoothly upward back into the clerestory vault.

Option 3 — Lead-Came Solder Weld [Join]: The meeting edges are bordered by authentic dark H-profile lead came channels holding jewel-toned stained glass quarry panes (liturgical ruby and cobalt on Jarvis, emerald and amethyst on GitDiscuss). Upon docking, a bright hot-iron point of light zips down the vertical channel, depositing a glowing molten silver bead that rapidly cools into a satiny pewter-silver soldered seam with three reinforcement rosettes. The stained glass panels unite across the seam into an unbroken narrative clerestory frieze. Pulling the center rosette releases the seam with a brief hot-flux melt glow.

Option 4 — Triptych Altar Fold [Shade]: Two hinged stone-relief side shutters carved with pointed lancet tracery and trefoils swing 90° inward over the clerestory altar face in 3D perspective, meeting at the center with a magnetic latch and glowing amber sanctuary jewel. The widget becomes a sealed stone reliquary casket with the glanceable title and status votives illuminated on its predella base. Unfolding releases the bronze clasp and swings the stone wings wide open, restoring full clerestory access.

Option 5 — Flying Buttress Arm Swing [Join]: A monumental arched stone flyer pivots 42° outward from the crocketed pier shoulder of Jarvis, swinging smoothly across the gap to seat into an acanthus-carved corbel on GitDiscuss with a forged iron tie-pin lock. The join preserves a load-bearing open-air architectural shadow gap between the clerestory walls. Pulling the corbel tie-pin releases the buttress arm.

Option 6 — Pillar Telescoping (Capital to Plinth) [Shade]: The monumental fluted stone columns flanking each display consist of three concentric, telescoping drums capped with carved Corinthian acanthus capitals and seated in molded stone plinths. When collapsing to shade mode, the plinths and lower casing smoothly slide upward while the fluted drums telescope inside one another and sink down into the plinth wells. Stone friction dust puffs burst at the collar bushings as the capitals seat with millimeter precision directly onto the plinths, compressing the module into a compact 44px slit framing the illuminated gold status plaque. Extending the pillars raises the column drums back to their full cathedral vault height.

Option 7 — Bronze Gargoyle Clench [Join]: A miniature cast-bronze grotesque / winged gargoyle with patinated verdigris finish is perched on the upper capital corbel of Jarvis. GitDiscuss features a heavy forged-iron tether ring set into an ashlar relief quatrefoil rosette boss. As the modules enter proximity, the gargoyle springs forward on its forged-iron hinge pin, its bat/dragon wings spreading wide across the clerestory roofline. Its muscular forepaws clamp tightly shut over the iron ring with a resounding metallic snap. A coiled bronze tail curling along the corbel serves as the release lever: pulling the tail right unclasps the claws to separate.

Option 8 — Rose Window Oculus Collapse [Shade]: An ornamental circular stone tracery diaphragm iris at the center of the capsule rotates clockwise through a 54° arc, causing 8 curved ashlar tracery blades to glide inward along logarithmic spiral paths like a cathedral camera iris. Each blade features a chiseled stone relief housing an authentic stained glass lancet quarry (liturgical ruby, celestial sapphire, cathedral emerald, and radiant topaz). As the aperture narrows, volumetric light streams through the opening before the blades lock with millimeter precision into a cast-bronze quatrefoil hub with a central glowing amber sanctuary jewel. The casing compresses to 44px height, forming a dense architectural lintel frieze flanked by miniature trefoil blind arcades and featuring three glowing 3D stained-glass cabochon gems (ruby, topaz, emerald). Expanding the rose window rotates the diaphragm counter-clockwise, sliding the stone leaves outward and restoring the full clerestory display.
