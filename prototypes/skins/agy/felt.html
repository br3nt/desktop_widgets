Tactile Felt Architecture, Fiber Entanglement, Accordion Bellows, Duffel Toggle, Tool-Roll, Cross-Stitch Zipper & Envelope Flap

This skin explores dense compressed wool felt (3mm–5mm thickness) with hand-sewn cotton embroidery stitches and turned boxwood fittings. The casing has pillowy, bevelled contours, diffuse ambient occlusion shadows, and visible needle-punched fiber texture.

Option 1 — Needle-Felt Fiber Entanglement [Join]: The docking edges feature loose, wispy carded wool roving fringes (sage green on Jarvis, warm terracotta on GitDiscuss). When the capsules are moved into proximity, the fibers reach across and interweave with rapid needle-punching indentations. On latch, the two bodies compress together with a cushioned squash, fusing into a seamless blended felt band reinforced by a column of tight ivory cross-stitches.

Option 2 — Accordion Bellows Pleat [Shade]: When collapsing to shade mode, scored horizontal fold lines in the heavy 3mm wool felt allow the body to accordion-fold flat. The pleats compress with lateral concertina gusset squish, revealing authentic textile thickness, ambient occlusion fold shadows, and alternating ivory running stitches and gold cross-stitches. A woven twill stay strap with a turned olive-wood duffle toggle pin fastens the compressed pleats into an ultra-compact 44px padded ribbon. The high-contrast status text and glanceable agent pips remain perfectly readable.

Option 3 — Duffel Toggle & Braid Cord [Join]: Module A features twin braided twisted-wool loop cords anchored by hand-sewn leather tabs. Module B carries twin hand-turned olive-wood toggle pins mounted on stitched leather patches with cross-stitched sinew. On docking, the braided cords reach across the gap, expand over the tapered toggle heads, and cinch taut into the carved waist grooves with a cushioned, muffled snap—creating a bespoke garment-style junction that holds the two plush felt casings securely together.

Option 4 — The Artist's Tool-Roll (Felt Scroll) [Shade]: Inspired by craftsman brush and needle rolls, the lower display bay and stitched tool organizer pockets curl smoothly upward like a heavy felt canvas scroll. As it rolls, the roll cylinder expands with Archimedean spiral cross-sections on its outer ends and longitudinal hand-sewn seams. At the summit, a bridle leather strap with an elastic twisted-wool cord loop stretches over a turned olive-wood craft button with a satisfying tactile snap, securing the widget into an ultra-compact 44px status strip with glanceable high-contrast text and pips.

Option 5 — Running Cross-Stitch Zipper [Join]: The joining flanks of both wool capsules feature pre-punched antique brass eyelets mounted on stiffened bridle-leather reinforcement welts. A thick, vibrant 6-strand scarlet embroidery floss threads through the eyelets from bottom to top, guided by a polished bone bodkin needle. As the modules draw together, the floss cinches tight into a continuous vertical column of interlocking cross-stitches (X X X), pulling the pillowy wool edges into a tight, dimpled seam. At the summit, the twin scarlet cords are tied into a hand-tied butterfly bow knot with turned brass aglet ferrules, and the bone needle seats beside it as an interactive release latch.

Option 6 — Envelope Flap Tuck [Shade]: Inspired by handcrafted artisan wool envelope clutches and travel wallets, the lower half of the widget features an embroidered hinge line reinforced by ivory running stitches and chevron corner gussets. When collapsing to shade mode, the lower envelope panel swings 180° upward around the hinge line, folding over the recessed display. At the peak of the fold, a shaped saddle-leather tongue tab with a polished brass eyelet rivet glides cleanly into a stitched horizontal clutch slot beneath the upper header with a cushioned fiber thwump. The widget collapses into an ultra-compact 44px felt envelope clutch displaying an unclipped, high-contrast status readout (Jarvis 03 Agents, GitDiscuss 02 Decisions), with the folded flap and tucked leather tongue proudly visible across the face. Clicking the tucked tongue or pressing 'E' smoothly untucks and unfolds the casing back to its full instrument height.

Option 7 — Heavy Brass Press-Stud (Popper) Snap [Join]: Drawing inspiration from heavy-duty industrial felt bags and rugged workwear garments, Module A (Jarvis) features twin stiffened bridle-leather tabs projecting from its right collar, each riveted with a precision-machined solid brass male popper stud featuring a knurled base flange and domed spring-ball head. Module B (GitDiscuss) carries complementary slotted pocket entries lined with circular ivory running stitches and recessed female brass sockets with internal tempered-steel split-ring clips. As the capsules draw together, the stiffened tabs slide into the slotted pockets with tactile friction. At docking threshold, the male studs snap decisively into the female sockets with a sharp "thwump-pop" and an elastic compression pulse, creating deep circular dimples in the plush wool padding around the visible brass snap rings. A vertical saddle-stitched leather reinforcement welt bridges the seam between the snaps. To release, grasping the leather pull tab at the seam and pulling right builds tension before popping the studs free with an elastic rebound.

Option 8 — High-Damp Wool Squeeze (Lozenge Squash) [Shade]: Exploring the physical squash-and-stretch viscoelasticity of dense carded wool roving under high virtual pressure, the entire 130px felt casing squashes vertically down to 44px while expanding laterally outward in a smooth, cushioned Poisson bulge into an ultra-compact rounded "lozenge pill" silhouette. The scalloped outer wool layers compress into thick, dense pillowy rims reinforced by tightly packed ivory blanket stitches. Twin curved bridle-leather compression cinch bands with polished brass rivets and turned olive-wood tension cleats wrap snugly around the bulging lateral lobes, visually and mechanically anchoring the compressed wool fibers. In the center face, a recessed dark pressed-charcoal felt cavity outlined in fine golden embroidery running stitches frames the high-contrast digital readout (Jarvis 03 Agents, GitDiscuss 02 Decisions) with generous margins so the status strip remains completely legible, crisp, and unclipped. Clicking the squashed lozenge body or pressing 'L' releases the compression pressure, smoothly returning the widget to its full instrument height.
