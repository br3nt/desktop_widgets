Claude skin study

Five aesthetics, built one option at a time. Every page keeps every option it has ever had. Ideas live in docs/skins/brainstorm/claude.md; the build order is docs/skins/progress/claude.md.

Wire, beads & stones

Oxidised copper wire frames with coil bands, coiled bezels around dark cabochon displays, bead buttons and bead meters.

  • Join: Jump ring hinge
  • Join: Bead strand bridge bridge, a wire feeds across the gap and status stones with gold spacers slide on one at a time like an abacus; a pearl drops in last and is the release
  • Join: Jump-ring pivot angle, the jump ring as a joint: GitDiscuss swings on the ring and settles at a set angle (−40° to +40°); drag its nameplate, use the slider or ↑ ↓ to swing it, with a copper dial and seed-bead detents at 15°, 30° or off
  • Join: Coil wrap bind, three wire ends leap across, hook GitDiscuss's spine and reel it in, then wind both spines together in three tight bands that anneal with a wave of heat; an amber bead is the join light; grab a band and pull to unwind it
  • Join: Twisted trunk fuse, three copper wires from Jarvis and three brass from GitDiscuss fan into coil collars, run across the gap past each other, then twist into one rope round a caged amber chip (the join light); the collars wind tight and the rope anneals; grab the rope and pull to untwist it
  • Join: Caged bead clasp, each end's flat watch-spring spiral (copper on Jarvis, brass on GitDiscuss) turns edge-on and draws out into a domed half-cage; a glass bead drops in and bounces, the halves close round it and twist shut with a spark, and the bead lights amber (labradorite when both are paused) as the join light; tap the bead to open the cage
  • Shade: Plain tuck page baseline
  • Shade: Bead strand distil, frame flattens to a wire, status stones slide on between a name slab and a value cabochon
  • Shade: Coil band cinch, the frame squeezes into a tube, copper wire winds it tight from both ends, and a riveted brass plaque drops on and is stamped with the name and value beside a status stone
  • Shade: Hoop rim sink, the display, plate and bezel coils sink back into the frame wire, leaving an empty copper outline; a single glass bead slides along the bottom rim with the value and a status glow, and a hot wire point writes the name
  • Shade: Folded branches fold, a three-strand trunk twists across the empty frame and sprouts stone-tipped branches like an open fern, then they fold toward the crown like umbrella ribs as the frame closes and fades; the stones line the trunk as statuses, brass wraps cinch it, and a name slab and value cabochon flip up
  • Morph: Tree growth grow. Checks changes form: a pendant, then a single cabochon on a bail, then a branch with two displays on an amethyst base, then a hoop tree with a crown display and fourteen bead tips, one per check. Its roots wrap onto a copper hoop that draws round, and the beads slide out along twigs to their new tips. Shrinking plays it backwards. Use the size buttons, M, or the slider.

Felt

Stuffed wool felt cushions with fuzzy edges and running stitch, cotton display patches, cross-stitch numbers and felt buttons. Chrome moves in 12 fps stop-motion.

  • Join: Zipper interlock
  • Join: Blanket stitch bind, chalk marks, then a needle works red floss down the seam one stitch a frame, pulls it tight so the felt puckers, and ties a French knot; scissors snip the knot and the thread is drawn out in one strand
  • Join: Cloth hinge angle, a pinked strip of oatmeal felt unrolls down the gap and is tacked on, then GitDiscuss flops over to any angle from −40° to +40°; the inside of the strip bunches into creases (more the further it bends) and the outside stretches pale with its teeth spread wide; drag its nameplate, use the slider or ↑ ↓, with a chalk arc and a cross-stitched tag showing the angle; tap the strip to unpick it
  • Join: Button tab clasp, a raspberry felt tab hanging off Jarvis perks up, flicks back and flops over onto a horn coat button on GitDiscuss, overshoots, and the button pops through the buttonhole with a squash and loose fibres; tug the tab (the felt stretches and drags the button until it pops free) to release
  • Join: Ribbon bow bind, a crimson satin ribbon with brass aglets hangs off Jarvis's bottom grommet; it lays a bar across the bottom, laces up the brass grommets criss-cross like a shoelace, pulls tight so the felt creases, and ties a bow whose loops pop out and tails swing; pull a tail and the loops shrink through the knot until it slips undone and unlaces
  • Join: Safety pin angle, a visible pivot: each cushion has a round felt ear, and a brass safety pin pinned through Jarvis's ear comes out, GitDiscuss's ear flops on top, and the pin goes back through both and clasps shut with a glint; GitDiscuss then swings round the pin from −30° to +30° with a detent every 15°, a red felt pointer reading the ticks stitched on Jarvis's ear (drag its nameplate, the slider or ↑ ↓); tap the pin to unpin it
  • Shade: Plain deflate page baseline
  • Shade: Pocket sink, the cushion drops into a contrasting felt pocket; a woven name tag peeks out and the value is cross-stitched under the mouth
  • Shade: Bolster roll, the cushion rolls up from its foot into a stuffed tube with a turning felt spiral at its end; satin ribbons tie it and bows pop, the name is satin-stitched and the value cross-stitched; unrolling slaps open with a bounce
  • Shade: Drawstring cinch, the name band slides down to become a casing, a cord drops through a wooden toggle, and three tugs gather the cushion into a strip with frilled, creased edges; the name is satin-stitched and the value cross-stitched on the band; pull a toggle down to let the gathers go
  • Shade: Pleats fold, dyed felt piping is drawn along each pleat's ridge, then the cushion folds up from the bottom into a concertina (lit and shaded panels, zigzag sides) until only the piping shows, stacked under the band; the piping colours are status (Jarvis three amber and one blue, GitDiscuss two red, one blue and one green, Checks one pleat per two checks, six green and one red); the name is satin-stitched and the value cross-stitched; opening springs past flat and settles
  • Morph: Stuffing inflate. Checks grows from a pad to a cushion to a pillow as wool tufts are pushed in through a slit in its side seam, each gulp puffing the felt; the slit is cross-stitched shut and it hops and lands with a squash. The cushion gets a bigger cross-stitched display and a row of 14 French knots, one per check; the pillow adds the run, a bar chart, the two waiting checks by name and corner pompoms. Shrinking lets the stuffing out in puffs while the felt sags and creases. Use the size buttons, M, or the slider.

Gothic cathedral

Weathered limestone facades with pinnacles, cresting and ashlar courses; displays are lit windows of clear quarry glass in coloured glass borders; carved and gilded bosses.

  • Join: Half-rose window interlock
  • Join: Flying buttress bridge, the facades stay a bay apart; timber centring rises, voussoirs drop in from both corbels, the keystone seats with a puff of dust and the centring is struck; lift the keystone to release
  • Join: Rose dial angle, the half-roses slide out to meet in the gap, a cusped stone wheel is set round them and a gilded pawl drops into a petal notch; turn the rose (or drag GitDiscuss, the slider or the arrow keys) and GitDiscuss swings half as far, each petal under the pawl a 15° detent, −45° to +45°; tap the pawl to release
  • Join: Lead came solder bind, each joining end carries half of a pointed centre light with a bare lead came; the halves meet as one window, a soldering iron runs molten solder down the came, puddling at each joint, and it cools from white heat to grey before the light comes through; draw the knife down the came to crack it open
  • Join: Compound pier fuse, each joining end is left toothed like an unfinished wall; the teeth mesh, a clustered pier is built over the seam course by course with a foliage capital and pinnacle, a niche is cut in it and a hooded, faceless lamp-bearer steps in and raises the shared lamp (amber while both run, cold blue when paused); take the lamp from its hand to release
  • Join: Apse wall angle, the facades close a narrow bay apart and a canted wall is laid course by course between them, with a crocketed gablet and a lit lancet; GitDiscuss turns in apse steps of 22.5° (±45°) or 30° (±60°), the wall cants half as far and widens to fit, and a mason's plan shows the bend; tap the finial to release
  • Shade: Plain lower page baseline
  • Shade: Clerestory distil, the wall lowers to a row of narrow lancets that kindle one by one, one lit window per status, under the carved name and value
  • Shade: Triptych fold, gilded altarpiece wings swing out and fold shut over the window; the closed wings carry the name and value in grey grisaille, and gilt turn-buttons lock them
  • Shade: Cornice sink, a cornice on a corbel table slides out under the frieze and the wall draws up behind it, display and all; crockets sprout along the top, a flat pointed slit in the cornice kindles with the key value, and the name is chiselled into the frieze letter by letter
  • Shade: Crypt sink, the display goes dark, ledger slabs rise round the foot, tracery is built up in the window into a row of pointed lights with jewel roundels that kindle, and the facade sinks into the floor (its foot stays put) until only the cresting, the frieze with name and value, and the glowing arch heads remain, their light leaking up the slot onto the floor
  • Morph: Build by bays grow. Checks grows from a single lancet to a triple lancet under a crocketed gable and small rose, then to a nave elevation with a clerestory of run bars, a triforium of fourteen lit lancets (one per check; hover or tap to read) and an arcade. Timber scaffolding goes up pole by pole, the new walls rise course by course round the old building while a gin wheel hoists stones, the windows are glazed and kindle, and the scaffold is struck in a puff of dust. Shrinking takes the walls down course by course. Use the size buttons, M, or the slider.

Hollow mood

Dark carapace shells with brush-ink outlines and scalloped belly plates, wrought-iron scrolls carrying small lanterns, iron eyelet straps, and parchment map sheets pinned on as displays, in front of foggy spires.

  • Join: Silk stitch bind
  • Join: Bell and chain clasp, a short chain stowed on Jarvis's strap swings across the gap and hooks the far eyelet; the brass bell rings and hangs in the open seam as the join light; tap it twice or pull the chain to release
  • Join: Hanging bracket angle, an iron knuckle hinges the shells, a notched quadrant arcs over the gap and a lamp bracket rises through it; GitDiscuss swings −30° to +30° in 10° notches while the lantern on the bracket hangs plumb and swings; tap the knuckle pin to release
  • Join: Carapace overlap interlock, thorned shell plates lift off Jarvis's strap and telescope across the gap, then drop over GitDiscuss's collar with a click; the seam is overlapping shell edges with dark gap lines; push the plates back toward Jarvis to unclick
  • Join: Ink bleed fuse, wet ink beads on each strap and threads reach across; the outlines run together, the gap floods, a splash flicks out and is pulled back on fine strands, and the ink dries into one shell with a shared outline, raised shoulders and a thorn over the old gap; pull the seam down to wet it, and it sags, pinches apart and draws back into each outline
  • Join: Shell articulation angle, three shell plates telescope out from under a thorned collar on Jarvis and click onto GitDiscuss; the joint flexes −45° to +45° in 15° detents, the plates sliding further under each other on the inside of the bend and fanning open on the outside over a creased, lamp-warmed membrane; drag the plates to flex, tap the lit spiracle to release
  • Shade: Plain draw-in page baseline
  • Shade: Cocoon cinch, white silk spins round the shell in two crossing passes and cinches it into a slim spindle; one lit seam opens with the name and value; hover or tap loosens the wrap
  • Shade: Map roll roll, the parchment rolls up from the bottom toward its pins, the pins pop out and the roll lifts onto the drawn-in shell; red thread ties it twice, a paper slip is pasted on and a quill writes the name and value
  • Shade: Hanging sign distil, the parchment hardens into a painted plank that shrinks to fit the name and value; an iron bar runs out from the scroll, two chains drop and the board falls onto them and swings level; it swings again whenever its value changes and sways as the shells move; tap a sign to pause or resume it
  • Shade: Curl roll, the shell splits into armoured plates, feels out with two antennae and paddles its legs, then the head tucks under and the front rolls into a tight ball like a pill bug; light kindles inside and leaks from every gap between the plates, with the name chalked on the back and the value on a pinned scrap
  • Morph: Map unfurl unroll, Checks' sheet is one corner of a larger map: growing to a folio slips the thread off a roll along its bottom and unrolls run 418 as a route of waymarks; growing to an atlas swings the folded half out from behind on its crease to name the two waiting checks beside a compass rose; shrinking rolls and folds it back

Elven

Long leaf-shaped casings of night glass with a pearl sheen and silver hairline rims, tapered filigree curling off each leaf tip, a crescent crest and moonstone drop, moonstone panes with pointed ends, script nameplates.

  • Join: Vine grow
  • Join: Leaf brooch clasp, eyelets curl out of each stem, a frosted leaf brooch drifts down like a falling leaf, its pin swings shut through both and the moonstone kindles; lift it off to release
  • Join: Branching angle, silver-birch twigs grow from both stems and fuse into a knot with a moonstone; a parent branch grows down with leaves set 137.5° apart, and GitDiscuss bends at the fork from −50° to +50° (drag its nameplate, the slider or ↑ ↓), settling softly on the golden angle (137.5°), ⅖ turn (144°) or a straight bough; click the knot to release
  • Join: Moonlight bridge bridge, the casings float a span apart; a moonbeam falls into the gap, light arches across from both stems and meets with a flare, then frosts into a silver rail and arch with spiral filigree, leaves and granules; a moonstone in a crescent cradle is the keystone and the join light; click it to release
  • Join: Runic seal interlock, each joining end carries a night-glass rune stave with four invented runes split down the middle; they kindle on approach, the staves slide out and meet, GitDiscuss's half-runes turn over like hinged pages until each completes its partner, light runs round the pointed seal and its moonstone kindles; the runes then fade and return on hover; slide the seal a rune up or down (or click it) to break the match
  • Shade: Plain narrow page baseline
  • Shade: Constellation distil, the leaf's outline pricks out into stars and flattens to one silver line; status stars kindle along it between the script name and the value
  • Shade: Leaf furl roll, the lower edge curls over and rolls up the casing into one slender furled leaf; its midrib lights as a progress line with buds for what is left, and a moonstone dewdrop at the tip holds the count
  • Shade: Lantern sleep sink, the light sinks out of the night glass and runs round the rim; the leaf sleeps as a lit outline in its status tone with the script name, one value and an ember pendant, and hover (or a tap) wakes it
  • Shade: Wing fold fold, the night glass clears into two veined translucent wings on a slim body; they quiver, then close like fans along their veins toward the tail and lie as thin blades above and below the body, with a status-lit pterostigma on each; the name and one value sit on the body
  • Morph: Growth grow, Checks grows from a bud to a sprig (a birch stem drops from its moonstone and a new leaf unfurls beneath, its pane showing run 418) to a bough (the branch grows on from the far tip, leafing, and a third leaf names the two waiting checks); shrinking is autumn: panes dim, leaves turn gold, fold and flutter down, and the branch draws back. Use the size buttons, M, or the slider

Combined skins

Experiments in mixing aesthetics. Each mix lists its layers (structure, material, ornament, palette, motion, display) and brings its own join and shade.

  • Mix: Felt cathedral layer stack, gothic structure in felt, stop-motion. Join: half-rose window in felt appliqué interlock. Shade: plain lower in felt page baseline
  • Mix: Stone & felt coupler half-coupler, a gothic stone Jarvis and felt GitDiscuss and Checks, each in its own skin. Join: stone boss and felt button tab clasp, the felt tab flops over a carved boss on a stone corbel and buttons on; tug it to release. Shade: plain lower, stone and felt page baseline
  • Mix: Reliquary bay containment, a gothic stone Jarvis holds felt GitDiscuss in a starry niche behind glass instead of joining edge to edge. Join: enshrined behind glass containment, the plinth slides out, GitDiscuss is set down, the pier rises, a canopy of crocketed gablets drops with dust, the glass lowers and gilt hasps lock it; pull the canopy up to release. Shade: plain lower, enshrined page baseline
  • Mix: Copper ivy on a pier pairwise seam, a gothic stone Jarvis with an engaged colonnette, and wire GitDiscuss and Checks, with a seam authored for this pair. Join: coil wrap round the pier bind, three copper strands leap across and wind round the stone shaft, tie off in curls holding stones (the amber one is the join light) and put out wire ivy leaves; pull the wire to unwind it. Shade: plain lower, stone and wire page baseline
  • Mix: Silver filigree layer stack, elven structure and ornament built from round silver wire: leaf casings as openwork frames of two wires packed with tiny scrolls, coil bindings, granules, a wire crescent crest, moonstone panes in coiled bezels. Join: vine in silver wire grow, wire springs on the knops uncoil and reach, two silver wires twist across the gap and coil round the far knops, moonstone beads ride out, enamelled wire leaves open and a filigree flower blooms with a kindling moonstone; pull the flower to release. Shade: plain narrow, in silver page baseline
  • Mix: Turned to stone dominant skin, Jarvis and GitDiscuss are one stone group with an oriel bay under their seam, and felt Checks takes the group's skin while it is docked. Join: turned to stone in the oriel dominant skin, Checks seats in the bay and a ripple spreads over it, turning the felt cushion into a limestone predella with its plum kept as the colour of its glass; pull the ring under the bay and the stone shell cracks and bursts off, leaving the felt as it was.   • Mix: Coil on a tendril half-coupler, an elven Jarvis (night glass and silver) and wire GitDiscuss and Checks, meeting at a shared rod face. Join: copper coil grips a silver tendril clasp, the curled silver tendril unfurls straight into an open copper spring, the spring twists shut on it a turn at a time with a hot working end, two leaves open at the coil's mouth and the moonstone collar kindles; pull the coil to unwind it. Shade: plain lower, elven and wire page baseline
  • Mix: Needle and red thread pairwise seam, a Hollow carapace Jarvis (iron eyelet strap, parchment map) and felt GitDiscuss and Checks, with a seam authored for this pair. Join: blanket stitch in red silk bind, the pale needle darts down the seam, passing through each brass eyelet with a spark and stabbing six blanket-stitch legs into the felt, which squashes and dimples; the thread pulls taut with a bounce, the felt puckers and the end is knotted; pull the thread and it comes out in one long strand. Shade: plain draw-in, shell and felt page baseline
  • Mix: Pocketed containment, a felt Jarvis with a brick-red patch pocket holds wire GitDiscuss; Checks is wire too. Join: dropped in, tag over the edge containment, the pocket unfolds out of Jarvis's end like a concertina, GitDiscuss is lifted across and dropped in, the lip folds its copper tag up, then the tag is laid over the edge, swings, and its amber bead kindles; tabs and bead buttons work through a blanket-stitched slot and buttonholes; pull the tag up to lift it out. Shade: plain tuck, pocketed page baseline
  • Mix: Ruined bug cathedral layer stack, gothic structure drawn in Hollow brush ink on navy chitin: thorn cresting and crockets, a snapped pinnacle with its shards at the foot, a lost run of cresting, cracks, shelf fungus, lancets and quarry borders as dim lit shell, one iron lantern, pinned parchment displays. Join: ruined half-rose, lit by glow-bugs interlock, the halves dart together and seat with ink flecks, the shell petals light from the top down and four glow-bugs drift in to settle where petals have fallen out; pull the rose to release. Shade: plain lower, in ruin page baseline
