# Claude skin study: progress

One item per run, in this order. Aesthetics are interleaved so none waits long. Pages: `prototypes/skins/claude/<skin>.html` and `combined.html`, with the index at `prototypes/skins/claude/index.html`.

- [x] **wire** · Join: jump ring (Hinge). Brass ring swings through eye loops on each end and twists shut; click the ring to open
  - Built in `wire.html`, which is a new page: copper wire frames, coiled cabochon displays and bead buttons. Loops warm when near, a ghost ring shows when ready, the ring threads and twists shut, and clicking it opens it again. A "plain tuck" baseline shade is included so the shade state works.
- [x] **felt** · Join: zipper (Interlock). Teeth lift on approach, the pull runs the seam; the pull is the release grip
  - Built in `felt.html`, a new page: filter-puffed felt cushions, cotton patches, cross-stitch digits. The teeth stand up when near and light when ready. The cushions lean into a V and the slider climbs it in 12 fps stop-motion. Drag the felt pull down (or click it, or press Enter) to unzip. A "plain deflate" baseline shade is included. Not yet checked in a browser.
- [x] **gothic** · Join: half-rose window (Interlock). Halves rotate into alignment; the full rose is the indicator; turn it to release
  - Built in `gothic.html`, a new page: limestone facades with pinnacles, cresting, half-gables at the joining ends and lit quarry-glass windows. The tracery turns into line on approach, the light blooms out from the boss and spills colour onto the stone, and you turn the rose 60° (or click it, or press Enter) to release. A "plain lower" baseline shade is included. Not yet checked in a browser (browser access was blocked this run).
- [x] **hollow** · Join: silk stitch (Bind). A needle darts red thread through eyelets and pulls taut; slash to release
  - Built in `hollow.html`, a new page: ink-outlined carapace shells with scalloped bellies, iron scrolls with lanterns (lit while running), brass-eyelet straps and pinned parchment displays. The eyelets glint when near, and on ready a needle appears with a ghost of the lacing. The needle darts two passes into a double cross, then pulls taut with a bounce and knots off. A slash across the seam (or a click, or Enter) cuts it with a white flash. A "plain draw-in" baseline shade is included. Not yet checked in a browser (browser and node access were blocked this run).
- [x] **elven** · Join: vine (Grow). Tendrils reach, spiral round each other and leaf out; drag apart to unwind
  - Built in `elven.html`, a new page: leaf-shaped night-glass casings with silver rims, tip filigree, a crescent crest and moonstone panes. Fiddlehead buds uncurl when near and their tips touch when ready. Tendrils grow into an over-and-under braid that curls round the opposite knops, leaves open along it, and a moonflower blooms. To release, pull a casing away (the braid strains, then lets go and unwinds) or click the flower. A "plain narrow" baseline shade is included. Not yet checked in a browser (browser and node access were blocked this run).
- [x] **combined** · Layer stack: felt cathedral (gothic structure, felt material, stop-motion)
  - Built in `combined.html`, a new page with a "Mix" switcher and a layer table per mix. These are stuffed felt kneelers with a gothic silhouette: half-gables, felt pinnacles with French-knot crockets, scalloped cresting, quilted ashlar stitching, appliqué glass borders and dark frieze nameplates. The join is the half-rose done in felt. Felt can't glow, so instead of lighting up, the rose has its petals sewn on clockwise in stop-motion after a squash that knocks out some fluff. Tacking stitches mark the ready state, and you turn the rose to unpick it. A "plain lower" baseline shade is included. Not yet checked in a browser (browser and node access were blocked this run).
- [x] **wire** · Shade: bead strand (Distil). Widget collapses into a necklace; stones are statuses, a flat tag bead carries the name
  - Added to `wire.html` as the "Bead strand" shade option. The plate fades, the copper frame squeezes flat into the strand wire, and the beads slide in from both clasps with a click: a slab bead for the name, round stones for statuses (amber running, labradorite paused, carnelian needs you, malachite passed), and a coiled cabochon for the key value. Eye loops stay, so shaded pendants still link by jump ring. Not yet checked in a browser (browser and node access were blocked this run).
- [x] **felt** · Shade: pocket (Sink). Widget slides into a felt pocket; a woven name tag and a cross-stitch value peek out
  - Added to `felt.html` as the "Pocket" shade option. A patch pocket in contrasting felt (denim, raspberry, moss) pops up over the cushion's foot, with a blanket-stitched mouth, a running-stitch seam and bar tacks. The cushion drops in with a squash, the pocket rises to the nameplate line, and a woven name tag pops out. The key value is cross-stitched in cream under the mouth. All of it runs at 12 fps with boiling fibres, and zipped pairs stay zipped. Not yet checked in a browser (browser and node access were blocked this run).
- [x] **gothic** · Shade: clerestory (Distil). A row of lit lancets, one per status, with tooltips
  - Added to `gothic.html` as the "Clerestory" shade option. The wall lowers to one storey under the carved frieze, which keeps the name and key value. Lancets rise out of the string course bay by bay, then kindle left to right with a small flare: amber running, blue idle or paused, red needs you, green passed. Empty bays stay blind. Each lit lancet glows onto its sill and has a parchment tooltip on hover, focus or tap; the tooltip is fixed-position, so it stays readable on a phone. Checks shows 14 lancets (12 green, 2 red). The rose join still works on the shaded pair. Not yet checked in a browser (browser and node access were blocked this run).
- [x] **hollow** · Shade: cocoon (Cinch). Thread spins the widget into a slim cocoon with one lit seam
  - Added to `hollow.html` as the "Cocoon" shade option. A point of white silk spins from the shell's outer tip in two crossing passes, cinching the boxy shell into a pointed spindle. The carapace fades under the silk, which is tethered to the iron scroll, pulls taut with a squeeze, and splits one seam open: amber while running, cold white when paused, sea-green for Checks. The name and key value sit in the seam in dark ink. Hover, keyboard focus or a tap loosens the wrap. The eyelet strap stays outside the silk, so cocooned pairs still lace. Not yet checked in a browser (browser and node access were blocked this run).
- [x] **elven** · Shade: constellation (Distil). A silver line with star points; each star a status
  - Added to `elven.html` as the "Constellation" shade option. The pane fades and star points prick out along the leaf's rim. The outline straightens into hairline chords and the night glass thins away. The figure flattens, tip first, into one silver line, and the crest's crescent drifts down to the tip. The line parts around the script name and the key value, and the status stars kindle one by one with a flare, stepping off the line into a zigzag. Jarvis has 3 white running stars (grey when paused) and 2 faint queued ones. GitDiscuss has 2 gold stars for decisions and 4 faint comment stars. Checks has 12 green and 2 gold. The joining stem stays, so shaded leaves still vine-join. Not yet checked in a browser (browser and node access were blocked this run).
